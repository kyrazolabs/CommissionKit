---
title: "Rep Portal"
description: "The admin-facing rep detail view"
---

The rep portal provides a detailed view of a single rep's performance, deals, and payouts. Access it from the reps list by clicking on a rep's name.

## Summary cards

The top section shows three summary cards:

- **Estimated Commission** — Total commission for the selected period
- **Total Revenue Closed** — Total deal revenue closed in the period
- **Deals Won** — Number of closed-won deals in the period

## Earnings history

A bar chart shows the past 6 months of commission payouts, giving a quick visual of earnings trends.

## Deal breakdown

A detailed table lists every deal for the selected period:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Deal | Deal name and calculation note |
| Close Date | When the deal closed |
| Amount | Deal amount (with currency conversion info) |
| Rate | Commission rate applied |
| Commission | Computed commission amount |

Multi-currency deals show the original amount and the converted workspace currency amount with the exchange rate snapshot.

## Payout history

The payout history section lists all payouts for the rep across all periods, showing:

- Period range
- Commission amount
- Adjustments
- Final amount
- Status
- Payment date
