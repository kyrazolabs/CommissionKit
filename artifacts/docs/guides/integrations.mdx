Connect CommissionKit to your ERP or CRM so reps and deals sync automatically — no manual entry needed. When deal data changes in your ERP, CommissionKit picks it up and recalculates commissions.

## Supported connectors

| Connector | What syncs | Auto sync |
|---|---|---|
| **Odoo ERP** | Reps (`res.users`) + Deals (`sale.order` from Sales or CRM app) | Yes — every 10 min, hourly, or daily |
| **Custom REST** | Any REST API — configure field mappings via JSON | Yes |

## How it works

```
Your ERP                      CommissionKit
─────────                     ────────────────

Rep creates deal               
       │
       ▼
Deal closed-won          →     Deal created (or updated if already synced)
       │
       ▼
Invoice created + paid   →     Deal payment status updates
       │
       ▼
Commission run           ←     Calculates commission from synced deals
```

Reps and deals sync **into** CommissionKit. Commission results and payout statuses optionally write **back** to the ERP.

## Payment status: paid vs unpaid

CommissionKit determines payment status by querying the actual invoices linked to each deal in your ERP. Here is exactly how it decides:

### Odoo

1. Fetches `sale.order` records with their linked `invoice_ids`
2. Queries each invoice's `payment_state` from `account.move`
3. Aggregates per deal:

| Invoice payment states | Resulting CKit status |
|---|---|
| All invoices `paid` or `in_payment` | **paid** |
| Some paid, some unpaid | **partial** |
| No invoices (not yet invoiced) | **unpaid** |
| Any invoice `reversed` or `cancel` | **on_hold** |

**Important**: `sale.order.invoice_status` is NOT used. That field only tracks whether invoices have been **created**, not whether they have been **paid**. CommissionKit queries the actual invoice records for accurate payment status.

### Other connectors

For the Custom REST connector, payment status mapping is configured via the `paymentStatusMapping` JSON field.

## Stages

Raw Odoo sale order states map to CommissionKit's canonical stages:

| Odoo state | CKit stage |
|---|---|
| `draft` | `pending` |
| `sent` | `pending` |
| `sale` | `closed_won` |
| `done` | `closed_won` |
| `cancel` | `closed_lost` |

By default, only `closed_won` deals (`sale` and `done`) are synced. Stages can be customized in the connector settings.

## Sync schedule

Choose how often CommissionKit pulls updates from your ERP:

| Option | Interval |
|---|---|
| **Every 10 min** | Fires a sync every 10 minutes |
| **Hourly** | Fires a sync every hour |
| **Daily** | Fires a sync every 24 hours |
| **Manual only** | No automatic sync — use the **Sync Reps** / **Sync Deals** buttons |

Manual syncs and auto-syncs behave identically: they fetch all records from the ERP and use hash-based change detection to skip unchanged data. A sync reports three counts:

- **New** — records that did not exist in CommissionKit
- **Updated** — records whose data changed in the ERP
- **Skipped** — records unchanged since the last sync

## Setting up

1. Go to **Integrations** in the sidebar
2. Select your connector
3. Enter credentials and click **Test Connection**
4. Click **Connect** to start the initial sync
5. Choose your auto-sync schedule from the **⋮** menu

Reps and deals sync immediately on connect. Changes in the ERP are picked up at the chosen interval.

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting stops all syncs but does **not** delete any data. All synced reps, deals, and commission history remain in CommissionKit.
