CommissionKit is a commission management platform that helps you track deals, define commission plans, and calculate payouts for your sales team.

## What you can do with CommissionKit

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Track Deals" icon="briefcase" href="/core-concepts/deals">
    Log and manage revenue events across your team with support for multiple currencies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Define Plans" icon="sliders" href="/core-concepts/plans">
    Create flat rate, tiered, or accelerator commission plans and assign them to reps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calculate Commissions" icon="calculator" href="/core-concepts/calculations">
    Run period-based calculations that auto-apply plans to deals and compute commissions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage Payouts" icon="wallet" href="/core-concepts/payouts">
    Review, approve, and payout commissions with full audit history and dispute resolution.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key features

- **Bulk import** — Upload CSV or XLSX files to import deals in bulk with preview and inline editing
- **Multi-currency** — Deals in different currencies are automatically converted using exchange rate snapshots
- **Flexible plans** — Flat rate, tiered (marginal brackets), and accelerator plans
- **Rep portal** — Give reps a password-protected portal to view their deals, commissions, and payout history
- **Dispute management** — Reps can dispute payouts; admins can review and resolve with notes
- **Audit trail** — Every calculation run is recorded with full breakdowns per deal and per rep
