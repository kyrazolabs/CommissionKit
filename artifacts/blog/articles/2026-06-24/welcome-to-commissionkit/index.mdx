## Hello, World

We are excited to launch CommissionKit — a platform purpose-built for sales commission management. After months of building, testing, and refining with early customers, we are ready to share what we have been working on.

## Why CommissionKit?

Managing sales commissions is surprisingly hard. Most teams rely on spreadsheets that are error-prone, opaque, and impossible to scale. Finance teams spend days at month-end reconciling numbers. Reps constantly ask "how much did I make on that deal?"

CommissionKit solves these problems:

- **Automated calculations** — Flat rate, tiered splits, accelerators, and custom engines
- **Real-time visibility** — Every rep gets their own portal to track earnings
- **Audit-ready** — Every calculation step is logged and traceable
- **Built for scale** — From 2 reps to 2,000, our platform grows with you

## What is Next

We are launching with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Over the coming weeks, we will share deep-dives into commission plan design, best practices for rep motivation, and real-world case studies.
