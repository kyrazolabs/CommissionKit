## We're live

If you've ever spent the last three days of the month reconciling commission spreadsheets, chasing down deal closures, arguing with reps over phone credits, manually cross-referencing CRM exports against finance's numbers , you already know the problem.

Commission management shouldn't feel like a monthly fire drill.

We built CommissionKit because we lived through the spreadsheet nightmare. Deals missed because the tier logic broke in row 847. Reps quitting because they didn't trust the payout math. Finance building a *second* shadow spreadsheet to audit the first one.

So we built something better.

## Why we built this

Sales commissions look simple but they're not. A flat 10% sounds easy , until you add tiered accelerators, multi-currency deals, split credit, clawbacks, and quarterly bonuses. Suddenly your model has 12 tabs, three pivot tables, and a VLOOKUP chain that nobody dares touch.

CommissionKit replaces all of that with one system:

- **Flexible commission plans** , flat, tiered, or accelerator structures, set up in minutes not days
- **Automated calculation runs** , import deals, hit calculate, get per-rep results with full audit trails
- **A transparent rep portal** , every rep sees exactly how their number was built, no more "trust me" conversations
- **Multi-currency with real exchange rates** , 170+ currencies, rate-snapshot history, auditable conversions
- **CRM and ERP integrations** , sync deals from Odoo, Salesforce, or HubSpot on a schedule, with change detection

The goal isn't just faster calculations. It's trust. When a rep can open their portal and see the exact formula, the exact deal, the exact rate that produced their number , and dispute it with one click if something looks off , the whole dynamic shifts. Finance stops being the bad guy. Reps stop guessing.
