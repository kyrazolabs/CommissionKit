# SEO and AEO Content Map

This implementation map translates the supplied discovery workbook into a buildable route plan. The workbook is a **discovery and planning artifact**, not a performance dataset: its volume, CPC, difficulty, ranking, and traffic fields are intentionally unavailable because no TrafficPeak, GSC, GA4, Ahrefs, Semrush, or DataForSEO data was connected. No forecast, traffic claim, ranking claim, or commercial outcome is implied by this map.

The first implementation tranche uses the existing public integration routes for verified product capability refreshes. New education and utility routes are delivered as **preview-only routes**: they must retain `noindex, nofollow` and remain out of the sitemap until product, editorial, legal, and accounting approval has been recorded. The intended country set for a later evidence-led validation is the **EU27**: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, and Sweden.

## Internal-Link Rules

Existing integration pages must link to their exact documentation page at `https://docs.commissionkit.co/integrations/{odoo|hubspot|salesforce}` and to relevant existing blog posts. Preview hubs may link to their child preview pages, the existing `/calculator`, `/features`, `/pricing`, and the appropriate integration route. They must not be included in the main sitemap, `llms.txt`, or primary navigation until publication approval changes their route state.

## Publication Gate

A route may move from preview to indexable only when the product owner confirms every capability statement, the content owner approves the copy, and any Finance, accounting, tax, legal, or comparison content has the required specialist review. Publication requires changing its central route state to `published`, adding it to the sitemap and `llms.txt`, confirming the prerendered canonical and robots tags, and re-running the SEO validation scripts.
