# Company Identity

## Name
CommissionKit

## Mission
Eliminate shadow accounting and commission disputes for sales teams.

## Vision
Every sales team has transparent, accurate, and fast commission management.

## Values

### 1. Accuracy over speed
Getting the number right matters more than getting it fast. A wrong commission calculation destroys trust.

### 2. Transparency builds trust
Reps should see exactly how their commission was calculated. No black boxes.

### 3. Reps deserve clarity
Sales reps are the revenue engine. They deserve to understand their earnings without begging finance for spreadsheets.

### 4. Automation replaces spreadsheets
Manual commission tracking in Excel is a bug, not a feature. We automate it.

## Brand Voice
- Expert practitioner — not corporate
- Clear and specific — no fluff
- First person: "I built", "we shipped"
- Real examples, not generic claims

## Target Market
- B2B companies with 5-100+ sales reps
- Currently tracking commissions in spreadsheets
- Revenue: $1M-$50M ARR
- Industries: SaaS, real estate, insurance, distribution, manufacturing, staffing, telecoms

## Customer Personas

### Admin / Finance
- Configures plans, runs calculations
- Approves payouts, resolves disputes
- Needs: accuracy, audit trail, efficiency

### Sales Manager
- Tracks team performance
- Runs reports, audits commissions
- Needs: visibility, fairness, motivation

### Sales Rep
- Views earnings, deal breakdowns
- Submits disputes, checks history
- Needs: transparency, speed, trust
