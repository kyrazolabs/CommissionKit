# Weekly Operating Rhythm

## Monday — Planning Day

**9:00 AM KSA — Weekly Kickoff (30 min)**
- Review last week's KPIs vs. targets
- Celebrate wins
- Identify blockers
- Set top 3 priorities for the week

**10:00 AM KSA — Content Planning**
- Select 2 blog topics for the week
- Fill out Content Briefs
- Review content calendar

**11:00 AM KSA — Sales Planning**
- Review CRM pipeline
- Identify 20 new leads for outreach
- Schedule discovery calls and demos

**2:00 PM KSA — Dev Standup**
- What shipped last week?
- What's in progress?
- Any blockers?

**4:00 PM KSA — Marketing Review**
- Review last week's social performance
- Plan week's social content
- Check SEO rankings

**Evening — AI Cron Runs (6 PM EST)**
- CK Daily Content generates next day's posts

---

## Tuesday — Execution Day

**9:00 AM KSA — Outreach Block**
- 10 LinkedIn connection requests
- 5 LinkedIn follow-up messages
- 10 cold emails

**11:00 AM KSA — Content Creation**
- Write first blog article draft
- Create carousel for LinkedIn

**2:00 PM KSA — Sales Calls**
- Discovery calls (target: 2)
- Demo calls (target: 1)

**4:00 PM KSA — Support Check**
- Review open tickets
- Respond to P1/P2 issues

---

## Wednesday — Mid-Week Check

**9:00 AM KSA — Mid-Week Standup (15 min)**
- Are we on track for weekly targets?
- Any emerging blockers?
- Do priorities need to shift?

**10:00 AM KSA — Reddit Post**
- Publish value-first post to r/salesops
- Engage with 3-5 relevant threads

**11:00 AM KSA — Sales Calls**
- Discovery calls (target: 2)
- Follow up with "engaged" leads

**2:00 PM KSA — Content Creation**
- Write second blog article draft
- Edit Monday's article for publish

**4:00 PM KSA — Finance Check**
- Review Stripe dashboard
- Check MRR movement
- Review failed payments

---

## Thursday — Push Day

**9:00 AM KSA — Outreach Block**
- 10 LinkedIn connection requests
- 5 LinkedIn follow-up messages
- Respond to all "contacted" leads from 48h ago

**11:00 AM KSA — Sales Calls**
- Discovery calls (target: 2)
- Demo calls (target: 1)

**2:00 PM KSA — Content Publish**
- Publish blog article #1
- Insert into Supabase (platform: blog)
- Share on social (or let cron handle)

**4:00 PM KSA — Product Work**
- Bug fixes or feature development
- Code review

---

## Friday — Review & Close Day

**9:00 AM KSA — Sales Close**
- Follow up with trials expiring in 4 days
- Send urgency emails
- Schedule weekend demos if needed

**11:00 AM KSA — Support Close**
- Clear all P1/P2 tickets
- Follow up on tickets awaiting customer response

**2:00 PM KSA — Weekly Retro (30 min)**
- What shipped this week?
- Biggest win?
- Biggest learning/failure?
- What moves to next week?

**3:00 PM KSA — Reporting**
- Update KPI dashboard
- Submit weekly sales report (if you have reps)
- Log commission calculations

**4:00 PM KSA — Content Publish**
- Publish blog article #2
- Insert into Supabase

**5:00 PM KSA — Shutdown**
- Clear inbox
- Set Monday priorities
- No Friday deployments (dev rule)

---

## Weekend — Light Touch

**Saturday/Sunday**
- AI cron runs automatically (1 X post each day)
- Monitor support inbox for P0 issues only
- Optional: engage on social, respond to comments
- No scheduled calls

## Daily Non-Negotiables

1. **Check Stripe** — every morning, 2 minutes
2. **Check support inbox** — every morning, 5 minutes
3. **Respond to social comments** — 2x daily, 10 minutes each
4. **Update CRM** — end of day, 5 minutes
5. **One sales activity** — every day (call, demo, or outreach)
