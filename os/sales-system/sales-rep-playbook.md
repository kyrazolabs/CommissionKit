# Sales Representative Playbook — CommissionKit

## Commission Structure

**First Invoice:** 30% of customer's first payment (monthly or annual)
**Recurring:** 10% of every subsequent payment for the lifetime of the account
**Extra Reps Add-on:** 10% of all extra rep charges ($8/rep/mo)
**Clawback:** If customer refunds within 60 days, commission is clawed back

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Must-Have:**
- B2B company with dedicated sales team (5+ reps)
- Currently tracking commissions in spreadsheets
- Revenue $1M–$50M ARR

**Strong Signals:**
- Hiring sales ops or commission analysts
- Recent sales team expansion
- Multi-currency deals
- Salesforce/HubSpot/Odoo user

**Industries:** SaaS, real estate, insurance, distribution, manufacturing, staffing, telecoms

**Pain Points to Probe:**
1. Reps doing "shadow accounting" in side spreadsheets
2. Commission disputes taking hours to resolve
3. FX rate errors on international deals
4. No visibility into earnings until payroll runs
5. Manual data entry from CRM to commission sheet

## Sales Scripts

### Discovery Call (15 min)

**Opening:**
> "Thanks for taking the time. Before I tell you about CommissionKit, I want to understand how you're handling commissions today. What's your current process?"

**Probe Questions:**
1. "How many reps are you tracking commissions for?"
2. "What tool are you using today — Excel, Google Sheets, or something else?"
3. "How often do reps come to you questioning their commission numbers?"
4. "Have you ever had a dispute that took more than a day to resolve?"
5. "Are you dealing with multiple currencies?"

**Transition to Demo:**
> "It sounds like [summarize pain point]. I'm going to show you exactly how CommissionKit solves that — and I'll time it, because the full setup takes under 10 minutes."

### Demo Script (20 min)

**1. Dashboard Overview (2 min)**
- Show workspace creation
- Highlight clean UI, speed

**2. Commission Plan Setup (5 min)**
- Create a tiered plan live
- Show clawback rules
- "This used to take an hour in a spreadsheet. Now it's 3 minutes."

**3. Deal Import (3 min)**
- Upload CSV or show CRM sync
- Highlight auto-stage mapping

**4. Commission Run (3 min)**
- Click "Run Commissions"
- Show async processing
- "500 deals processed in 4 minutes."

**5. Rep Portal (3 min)**
- Show the transparency view
- "No more Slack DMs asking 'are my numbers right?'"

**6. Payout & Disputes (3 min)**
- Show approval workflow
- Show dispute resolution

**7. Close (1 min)**
- "Which plan fits your team size?"
- If hesitation: "Let's start with a 14-day trial. I'll check in on day 3 to make sure your first plan is set up."

### Objection Handling

**"We already have a system."**
> "Most companies do — usually a spreadsheet. The question is: does it scale? When you add your 10th rep, will that spreadsheet still work?"

**"Too expensive."**
> "One commission dispute costs more than a month of CommissionKit. How many hours did your team spend on commission math last month?"

**"We need to evaluate other options."**
> "Absolutely. Here's what I'd suggest: start the trial, set up one plan with real data, run one commission calculation. If it doesn't save you time, cancel. No hard feelings."

**"We need custom commission logic."**
> "That's exactly why we built custom engines. We don't force you into a template — we build an engine that matches your business model. Can you share your current commission structure?"

**"I need to check with my CFO/CEO."**
> "Of course. I'll send you a one-page summary and a Loom walkthrough you can forward. When should I follow up — Tuesday or Thursday?"

## CRM Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Definition | Action | Timeframe |
|-------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Lead | Contact identified | Qualify, add to CRM | Day 0 |
| Contacted | First outreach sent | Wait 48h | Day 0-1 |
| Engaged | Replied or clicked trial | Book demo or send follow-up | Day 1-3 |
| Trial Active | Signed up for trial | Day 3 & Day 7 check-in | Day 3-14 |
| Trial Expiring | Day 10-14 | Urgency email + personal outreach | Day 10-14 |
| Converted | Paid subscriber | Welcome email, onboarding | Day 14+ |
| Churned | Cancelled | Exit survey, win-back at 30 days | Ongoing |
