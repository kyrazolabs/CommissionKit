# Social Engage Skill

Social media engagement rules.

## When to Use

- X (Twitter) threads
- LinkedIn posts
- Reddit comments
- Community participation
- Trend-jacking

## Method

1. Be helpful first, promotional second (90/10 rule)
2. Reply to 5 posts before posting your own
3. Use native formatting (no cross-post spam)
4. Tag relevant people sparingly
5. Track engagement and iterate weekly
