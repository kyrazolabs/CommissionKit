# Sales Outreach Skill

Outreach methodology and objection handling.

## When to Use

- Cold emails
- LinkedIn messages
- Discovery calls
- Demo follow-ups
- Closing sequences

## Method

1. Research prospect before contact
2. Personalize first line (specific trigger)
3. Lead with pain point, not product
4. Clear CTA (low friction)
5. Follow-up 3-5 times with value-add each time
