# Research Skill

Systematic web research with source verification.

## When to Use

- Competitive analysis
- Market sizing
- ICP research
- Technology evaluation
- Pricing research

## Method

1. Define research question
2. Identify 3-5 authoritative sources
3. Extract data with citations
4. Cross-verify conflicting claims
5. Synthesize into actionable summary
