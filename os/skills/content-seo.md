# Content-SEO Skill

SEO-optimized content creation.

## When to Use

- Blog posts
- Landing page copy
- Lead magnets
- Newsletter content
- Help center articles

## Method

1. Keyword research (primary + secondary)
2. Outline with H2/H3 structure
3. Write for humans first, optimize for search second
4. Internal links to relevant product pages
5. Meta title + description under 160 chars
