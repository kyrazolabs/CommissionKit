# SOP-04: Sales Calls

## Purpose
Standardize discovery calls and demos to maximize conversion from first conversation to trial signup.

## Call Types

### Discovery Call (15 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand their commission pain and qualify BANT.
**When:** After lead engages but before demo.

**Agenda:**
1. **Rapport (2 min):** "How's your week going? What's keeping you busy in sales ops right now?"
2. **Current State (5 min):** "Walk me through how commissions work at [Company] today."
   - How many reps?
   - What tool?
   - How often do reps question their numbers?
   - Any multi-currency deals?
3. **Pain Probe (5 min):** "If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about commissions, what would it be?"
   - Quantify the pain: "How many hours per month does your team spend on commission math?"
4. **Qualification (2 min):** BANT check
5. **Next Steps (1 min):** "Based on what you told me, I'd love to show you how CommissionKit handles [specific pain]. Can we book a 20-minute demo this week?"

**Red Flags (disqualify):**
- Under 5 reps
- No commission plan (everyone on salary)
- "We're fine with spreadsheets"
- No decision-making authority + won't introduce us

### Demo Call (20 minutes)
**Goal:** Show CommissionKit solving their exact pain. Get them to start a trial.
**When:** After discovery call or hot inbound lead.

**Agenda:**
1. **Confirm Pain (2 min):** "Last time you mentioned [pain]. That's what we're solving today."
2. **Dashboard Overview (2 min):** Show workspace, clean UI
3. **Plan Setup (5 min):** Create their commission structure live
   - "What does your current plan look like?" → Build it in real time
4. **Deal Import (3 min):** Upload sample data or show CRM sync
5. **Commission Run (3 min):** Click run, show results
6. **Rep Portal (3 min):** "This is what your reps see. No more Slack DMs."
7. **Close (2 min):** "Which plan fits your team? Let's get you on a 14-day trial. I'll check in on day 3 to make sure your first plan is running."

**Demo Rules:**
- Never demo without knowing their pain first
- Always build THEIR plan, not a generic one
- Time the demo: "This whole setup takes under 10 minutes"
- If they ask about a feature we don't have: "That's on our roadmap. Let me note that down and follow up."

## Call Follow-Up

**Within 1 hour:**
Send email with:
1. Thank you + summary of their pain
2. Loom recording of demo (if applicable)
3. Trial link with their plan pre-configured (if possible)
4. Calendly link for next call

**Template:**
```
Subject: [Company] commission setup — next steps

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time today. To recap:
- [Their pain point]
- [Their team size + plan type]
- [Next step: trial or follow-up call]

Here's your trial link: [URL]

I'll check in Wednesday to see how your first commission plan is looking.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Call Recording
- Record all demos with Loom
- Store recordings in shared folder
- Review 1 recording per week for coaching

## KPIs
- Discovery calls per week (target: 10)
- Demo calls per week (target: 5)
- Discovery → Demo rate (target: 50%)
- Demo → Trial rate (target: 60%)
- Average call duration (track trends)
