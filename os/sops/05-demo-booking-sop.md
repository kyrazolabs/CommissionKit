# SOP-05: Demo Booking & Preparation

## Purpose
Ensure every demo is well-prepared, well-attended, and maximizes conversion to trial.

## Booking Process

### Step 1: Offer Times
Present 2-3 specific time slots. Never say "when are you free?"

**Example:**
> "I have Tuesday at 10 AM KSA or Wednesday at 2 PM KSA open. Which works better?"

### Step 2: Send Calendar Invite
Include in invite:
- Zoom/Meet link
- Agenda ("20-min demo: CommissionKit for [Company]")
- "Please come prepared to share your current commission structure"

### Step 3: Reminder Sequence
- **24 hours before:** Email reminder with agenda + prep questions
- **1 hour before:** SMS or WhatsApp reminder (if number available)
- **5 minutes before:** Join call early, test screen share

## Pre-Demo Research (10 minutes)

Before every demo, research:
1. **Company website** — industry, size, products
2. **LinkedIn** — their role, how long they've been there
3. **Current tools** — any tech stack signals (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
4. **Pain signals** — job postings mentioning sales ops, commission analysts

**Prep Notes Template:**
```
Company: [Name]
Industry: [Industry]
Size: [Employees]
Current tools: [CRM/ERP if known]
Likely pain: [Based on research]
Custom engine needed? [Yes/No/Unsure]
Plan to recommend: [Starter/Growth/Pro]
```

## Demo Environment Setup

1. **Clean workspace:** Fresh CommissionKit workspace, no test data clutter
2. **Pre-built plan:** Have their likely commission structure ready to show
3. **Sample data:** 10-20 deals loaded, ready to run
4. **Screen share ready:** Close all irrelevant tabs
5. **Backup plan:** If internet fails, have Loom recording ready to share

## No-Show Protocol

**If they don't join within 5 minutes:**
1. Send message: "Hi [Name], I'm in the Zoom room. Let me know if you need the link resent."
2. Wait 10 minutes total
3. Send follow-up: "Sorry we missed each other. Here's a 5-minute Loom walkthrough of what I was going to show you: [link]. Want to reschedule?"

## Post-Demo Actions

**Within 1 hour:**
- Send follow-up email (see SOP-04 template)
- Update CRM: demo completed, notes, next step
- If trial started: add to Day 3 check-in sequence
- If no trial: schedule follow-up in 48 hours

**Within 24 hours:**
- If no response: send one more follow-up with case study
- If objections raised: send specific content addressing objection

## KPIs
- Demo no-show rate (target: <20%)
- Demo → trial rate (target: 60%)
- Average time from demo to trial start (target: <24 hours)
