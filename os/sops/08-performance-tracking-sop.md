## Weekly Review Schedule

### Monday Morning (Management + All Teams)
**Time:** 9:00 AM KSA
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Agenda:**
1. Review last week's KPIs vs. targets (5 min)
2. What shipped? What didn't? Why? (10 min)
3. Top 3 priorities for this week (10 min)
4. Blockers that need help (5 min)

### Wednesday Mid-Week Check
**Time:** 2:00 PM KSA
**Duration:** 15 minutes

**Agenda:**
1. Are we on track for weekly targets? (5 min)
2. Any blockers emerging? (5 min)
3. Do priorities need to shift? (5 min)

### Friday Weekly Retro
**Time:** 4:00 PM KSA
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Agenda:**
1. What shipped this week? (5 min)
2. What was the biggest win? (5 min)
3. What was the biggest learning/failure? (5 min)
4. What moves to next week? (5 min)
5. Update SOPs if processes changed (10 min)

## Monthly Business Review

**Time:** First Monday of the month, 9:00 AM KSA
**Duration:** 2 hours

**Agenda:**
1. Financial review (30 min)
   - MRR, churn, expansion, net revenue
   - Expenses, gross margin, runway
2. Product review (20 min)
   - What shipped? What's in progress?
   - Customer feedback themes
   - Bug/backlog review
3. Marketing review (20 min)
   - Traffic, conversions, content performance
   - Channel ROI
4. Sales review (20 min)
   - Pipeline health, rep performance
   - Objection trends, competitive intel
5. Support review (10 min)
   - Ticket volume, satisfaction, recurring issues
6. Roadmap prioritization (20 min)
   - What builds next month?
   - Decision framework (see Decision Matrix)

## Decision-Making Framework

For any major decision (new feature, pricing change, hire, partnership):

1. **Does this move us closer to our next MRR milestone?**
2. **Does it improve retention or reduce churn?**
3. **Can we measure its impact within 30 days?**
4. **What is the cost in time and money vs. expected return?**
5. **What is the risk if it fails?**

**Score:** 3+ yes answers + manageable risk = proceed. Otherwise, delay and revisit next month.
