# SOP-03: Lead Generation

## Purpose
Systematically identify, qualify, and reach out to potential CommissionKit customers.

## Channels

### 1. LinkedIn Outreach (Primary)
**Target:** Sales Ops, RevOps, CFOs, VPs of Sales at B2B companies 5-100 reps
**Volume:** 20 connection requests + 10 follow-up messages per day
**Tools:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo.io, Hunter.io

**Message Sequence:**
1. **Connection request:** Personalized note referencing their role or recent post
2. **Follow-up (48h):** Value-first message about a common pain point
3. **Follow-up (4 days):** Soft pitch with trial link
4. **Follow-up (7 days):** Case study or testimonial
5. **Nurture (90 days):** Monthly valuable content, no pitch

### 2. Cold Email (Secondary)
**Target:** Same as LinkedIn, but with verified work emails
**Volume:** 50 emails per week
**Tool:** Apollo.io, Instantly.ai

**Rules:**
- Personalize first line with company-specific insight
- Subject line: question or curiosity gap (not "CommissionKit demo")
- Max 3 follow-ups, 3 days apart
- Unsubscribe link on every email
- Never send to generic emails (info@, support@)

### 3. Reddit (Inbound)
**Target:** r/salesops, r/sales users asking about commission tools
**Volume:** 3-5 helpful comments per week
**Rules:**
- Only respond to posts asking for help
- Disclose: "I built CommissionKit to solve this exact problem"
- Link naturally, never drop links in unrelated threads

### 4. Inbound (Website)
**Sources:** SEO blog traffic, Product Hunt, referrals
**Action:** All trial signups get Day 0 welcome email + Day 3 check-in

## Lead Qualification (BANT)

| Criteria | Question to Ask | Minimum Threshold |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Budget | "What's your current spend on commission tools?" | $49+/mo possible |
| Authority | "Who approves new software purchases?" | Can introduce to decision maker |
| Need | "How are you tracking commissions today?" | Spreadsheets or manual |
| Timeline | "When do you want to have this solved?" | Within 90 days |

**Score:** 3+ criteria met = hot lead. 2 criteria = warm. <2 = nurture.

## Daily Routine

### Morning (9 AM KSA)
1. Check CRM for new trial signups from overnight
2. Send Day 3 check-in emails to trials started 3 days ago
3. Send 10 LinkedIn connection requests

### Afternoon (2 PM KSA)
1. Send 5 LinkedIn follow-up messages to "contacted" leads
2. Send cold email batch (10-15 emails)
3. Respond to Reddit mentions/comments

### End of Day
1. Update CRM with all interactions
2. Log any new leads discovered
3. Check trials expiring in 4 days — schedule personal outreach

## CRM Requirements
Every lead must have:
- Source (LinkedIn, email, Reddit, inbound)
- Qualification score (hot/warm/cold)
- Last contact date
- Next action + date
- Notes from all interactions

## KPIs
- Leads contacted (weekly)
- Reply rate (target: 15%+)
- Discovery calls booked (weekly)
- Trial signups from outreach (weekly)
- Cost per lead (if using paid tools)
