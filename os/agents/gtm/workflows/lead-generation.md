# Lead Generation Workflow

**Owner:** @scout  
**Team:** GTM

## Steps

1. **Define ICP** — Review target criteria (5-100 reps, spreadsheet-bound, B2B)
2. **Research** — Use LinkedIn, Crunchbase, and web search to find companies
3. **Score leads** — Apply BANT framework (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
4. **Enrich data** — Add tech stack, company size, decision makers
5. **Document** — Write structured research notes per company
6. **Route to Clutch** — Pass qualified leads for outreach

## Lead Scoring

| Signal | Weight | Example |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Sales ops job posting | High | "Hiring: Sales Operations Manager" |
| Mention of spreadsheets | High | "Track commissions in Excel" |
| 5-100 sales reps | Medium | LinkedIn headcount |
| $1M-$50M ARR | Medium | Crunchbase data |
| CRM implementation | Low | HubSpot/Salesforce customer |
