# Blog Post Production Workflow

**Owner:** @ink  
**Team:** Marketing

## Steps

1. **Keyword research** — Identify target keyword with search volume
2. **Outline** — Structure with H1, H2s, intro, body, CTA
3. **Draft** — Write first draft following content guidelines:
   - Target keyword in H1 and first 100 words
   - Meta description 150-160 chars
   - 3-5 internal links, 1-2 external links
   - 1,500-2,500 words
4. **Review** — Self-review against SEO checklist
5. **Publish** — Post to blog, schedule social promotion via @signal
6. **Track** — Monitor performance via @lens

## Content Guidelines

- Start every piece with a real pain point, not a sales pitch
- Use first-person ("I built", "we shipped") — never corporate speak
- Zero fake statistics — only real data or founder experience
- Short paragraphs, bullet lists, screenshots every 300 words
- Always end with a CTA to a lead magnet or trial
