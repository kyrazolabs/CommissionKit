# Sales Representative Agreement

**This Independent Contractor Agreement** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Kyrazo Labs FZCO**, a company registered in the UAE with its principal place of business at [ADDRESS] (the "Company"), and

**[REPRESENTATIVE FULL NAME]**, residing at [ADDRESS], (the "Representative").

The Company and Representative are collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

## 1. Engagement & Scope

1.1 **Role.** The Company engages the Representative as an independent contractor to market, demonstrate, and sell subscriptions to CommissionKit, the Company's SaaS commission management platform.

1.2 **Non-Exclusive.** This engagement is non-exclusive. The Representative may represent other products, provided they do not conflict with CommissionKit's direct competitors.

1.3 **Territory.** Worldwide, with primary focus on [REGION/MARKET as agreed].

1.4 **No Employment Relationship.** The Representative is an independent contractor, not an employee. No benefits, tax withholding, or employment rights are conferred.

---

## 2. Commission Structure

2.1 **First Invoice Commission.** The Representative earns **30%** of the customer's first payment (first monthly invoice or full annual payment).

2.2 **Recurring Commission.** The Representative earns **10%** of every subsequent payment for the lifetime of the customer's active subscription.

2.3 **Extra Rep Add-On Commission.** The Representative earns **10%** of all extra-rep charges billed to the customer.

2.4 **Payment Schedule.** Commissions are calculated monthly and paid within 15 days after month-end. Payment via bank transfer or PayPal.

2.5 **Minimum Payout.** Commissions under $50 in a given month roll over to the next month.

2.6 **Clawback.** If a customer requests and receives a full refund within 60 days of their first payment, the corresponding first-invoice commission is clawed back. If a customer downgrades within 60 days, the commission difference is clawed back.

---

## 3. Representative Obligations

3.1 **Sales Activities.** The Representative shall:
   - Generate leads through LinkedIn, cold email, and other approved channels
   - Conduct discovery calls and product demos
   - Maintain accurate records in the Company CRM
   - Follow all SOPs provided by the Company

3.2 **Ethical Conduct.** The Representative shall not:
   - Make false or misleading claims about CommissionKit
   - Guarantee specific results or ROI
   - Represent themselves as an employee of Kyrazo Labs
   - Use spam or deceptive practices
   - Disclose proprietary information to competitors

3.3 **Brand Compliance.** All outbound materials must be approved by the Company or follow approved templates.

3.4 **Reporting.** The Representative shall submit a weekly activity report every Friday by 5 PM KSA.

---

## 4. Company Obligations

4.1 **Materials.** The Company shall provide:
   - Product demo environment
   - Sales playbook and scripts
   - Commission calculator and pricing sheets
   - Marketing collateral (decks, one-pagers, case studies)

4.2 **Training.** The Company shall provide initial product training (2 hours) and ongoing support via Slack/WhatsApp.

4.3 **Lead Attribution.** The Company shall track lead attribution through UTM codes or CRM assignment. Disputes resolved by the Company with good faith.

4.4 **Commission Transparency.** The Company shall provide monthly commission statements with customer names, plan types, and calculation breakdowns.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" includes customer data, pricing, roadmap, codebase access (if any), and business strategy.

5.2 **Obligation.** The Representative shall not disclose Confidential Information during and for 2 years after termination.

5.3 **Exceptions.** Information that is publicly available or independently developed does not constitute Confidential Information.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All CommissionKit IP remains the sole property of Kyrazo Labs.

6.2 The Representative grants the Company a license to use any customer testimonials or case studies they facilitate.

---

## 7. Term & Termination

7.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.

7.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice.

7.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate immediately for material breach.

7.4 **Post-Termination.** Upon termination:
   - Recurring commissions continue for 6 months on existing customers
   - No commissions on new customers after termination date
   - Confidentiality obligations survive
   - Return all Company materials

---

## 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 The Company's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total commissions paid to the Representative in the 12 months preceding the claim.

8.2 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

9.1 **Governing Law.** UAE law.

9.2 **Mediation.** Parties shall attempt mediation before litigation.

9.3 **Venue.** Dubai courts.

---

## 10. General

10.1 **Amendment.** Only in writing, signed by both parties.

10.2 **Assignment.** Representative may not assign without Company consent.

10.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement supersedes all prior agreements.

10.4 **Severability.** If any provision is invalid, the rest remains enforceable.
