## Revenue Model
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Annual subscriptions (save 17%)
- Add-on: extra reps beyond plan limit
- No setup fees, no contracts

## Sales Motion

### Phase 1: Founder-Led Sales (Now)
- Founder does demos and closes first 10 customers
- Agents support: Scout researches, Clutch writes outreach
- Goal: Learn what resonates, refine pitch

### Phase 2: Product-Led Growth (Month 4+)
- Self-serve trial with in-app onboarding
- Content drives inbound leads
- Agents handle scale: more leads, more content, more support

### Phase 3: Sales-Led + Product-Led (Month 9+)
- Hire first human sales rep (or promote Clutch workflow)
- Enterprise deals with custom engines
- Bridge partnerships drive referrals
