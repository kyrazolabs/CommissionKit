# CommissionKit — Enterprise Sales Playbook
### From First Touch to Custom Engine Delivery

---

## Overview

This playbook covers the full lifecycle of landing an enterprise client on CommissionKit — from the first LinkedIn message to delivering their custom commission engine and documentation. Follow each phase in order.

---

## Phase 1: Prospecting — Who to Target

### Ideal Client Profile
- B2B companies with a dedicated sales team (5+ reps)
- Industries: SaaS, real estate, insurance, distribution, manufacturing, staffing, telecoms
- Pain signal: still managing commissions in Excel or Google Sheets
- Revenue range: $1M–$50M ARR (large enough to feel the pain, small enough to move fast)

### Where to Find Them
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator: filter by job title "Sales Operations", "Revenue Operations", "CFO", "VP Sales"
- Look for companies hiring "Sales Ops Analyst" or "Commission Analyst" — that's a clear pain signal
- Job boards (LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed): search "commission reconciliation", "sales incentive management"
- Niche communities: RevOps communities, Slack groups (RevGenius, Sales Hacker)

---

## Phase 2: LinkedIn Outreach — First Touch

### Who to Connect With
Target **one of these three** at the company (in order of priority):
1. Head of Sales Operations / Revenue Operations
2. CFO or Finance Director
3. VP of Sales

### Connection Request Note (keep it under 300 characters)
> "Hi [Name] — I build sales commission automation tools for [industry] teams. Noticed [Company] is scaling its sales org. Would love to connect and swap notes."

### Follow-Up Message (send 2–3 days after connecting)
> "Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick question — how does your team currently handle commission calculations at the end of each month? I ask because most sales ops teams I talk to are still doing it in spreadsheets, which gets painful fast. Curious if that's the case for you too."

**Goal of this message:** Get them to say "yes, we use spreadsheets" or describe their current pain. That's your opening.

### If They Engage — The Discovery Prompt
> "That makes sense. We've seen that a lot. Would you be open to a 20-minute call? I'd love to understand your current setup and show you how we've helped similar teams cut commission processing time by 80%+."

---

## Phase 3: Discovery Call — Understanding Their Problem

### Call Structure (20–30 minutes)

**Opening (2 min)**
> "Thanks for making time. My goal today is just to understand how you currently manage commissions — no pitch, just questions. If there's a fit, we can talk about that at the end."

**Discovery Questions**

*Current process:*
- "Walk me through how you calculate commissions today, end to end."
- "What tools are you using? Excel, a specific software, something custom?"
- "How long does it take your team to run commissions each month?"

*Pain points:*
- "Where do errors or disputes usually come from?"
- "Have reps ever come to you questioning their payout? How often?"
- "What happens when a deal gets cancelled or adjusted mid-month?"

*Scale:*
- "How many sales reps are you calculating for?"
- "How many deals per month roughly?"
- "Do you have different commission structures per rep or team?"

*Commission structure specifics:*
- "Is it a flat % per deal, or does it change based on deal size or margin?"
- "Do you use tiers, accelerators, or bonuses?"
- "Do you have multi-currency deals?"

*Decision and urgency:*
- "Is fixing this on the roadmap for this quarter, or is it more exploratory?"
- "Who else would be involved in evaluating a solution like this?"

### What You're Listening For
| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "We spend 3+ days on commissions" | High urgency |
| "Reps dispute their numbers constantly" | Trust problem — strong pain |
| "Our structure is complex / custom" | Good fit for custom engine |
| "We've looked at [tool X] but it doesn't fit" | Standard tools failed them |
| "The CFO is involved" | Budget exists |

## Phase 5: The Proposal

### Positioning CommissionKit

Don't sell features. Sell the outcome:

> "Based on what you shared, your team is spending [X days] per month on commission calculations and dealing with [Y disputes] per quarter. We'll eliminate both — fully automated, with a rep portal so every salesperson can see exactly what they've earned in real time."

### Pricing Structure (Suggested)

| Component | What It Is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom Engine Setup** | One-time build of their commission logic into CommissionKit | $500–$1,500 (one-time) |
| **Platform Subscription** | Monthly access to CommissionKit (runs, portal, exports, disputes) | $99–$299/mo |
| **Onboarding** | Data migration, rep setup, matrix config | Included or $200 one-time |

**Anchor the value:** If their team spends 3 days/month on commissions at an ops salary of $5K/month, that's ~$750/month in labor wasted. CommissionKit pays for itself in month one.

### Proposal One-Pager Structure
1. Their problem (in their words from the discovery call)
2. Their commission structure summary (shows you listened)
3. What CommissionKit does for them specifically
4. Pricing
5. What happens next (simple 3-step onboarding)

## Phase 6: Closing

### After Sending the Proposal
Follow up in 2 business days:
> "Hi [Name] — wanted to check if you had a chance to look at the proposal. Happy to jump on a quick call to walk through it or answer any questions."

### Handling Common Objections

**"We don't have budget right now."**
> "Understood. When would be a better time to revisit? I can also show you a lighter starting point if that helps get things moving."

**"Our commission structure is too complex."**
> "That's actually exactly the use case we're built for. The engine is fully custom — we configure it around your exact logic, not the other way around."

**"We already looked at [Salesforce / CaptivateIQ / Spiff]."**
> "Those are great tools for large enterprises, but they're expensive and take months to implement. We're purpose-built and can have you live in a week."

**"Can we try it first?"**
> "Absolutely. I can set up a demo environment with your commission structure so you can see exactly how it would work for your team before committing."

### The Close
> "If the proposal looks good, the next step is simple — I'll set up your workspace, we'll configure your commission matrix together, and you'll be running your first automated commission cycle within a week. Want to get started?"

## Phase 7: Onboarding Delivery

Once the deal is signed, follow this delivery flow:

### Week 1 — Setup
1. Create workspace in CommissionKit
2. Set `commissionEngine` based on their structure type
3. Configure commission matrix (slabs, brackets, rates) based on their document
4. Add reps
5. Test run with historical data

### Week 2 — Handoff
1. Bulk import their existing deals
2. Run first commission cycle together on a call
3. Share rep portal links with their sales team
4. Deliver their **Commission Structure Document** (see below)

### Deliverable: Commission Structure Document

After onboarding, deliver a clean PDF/Markdown doc that explains:
- How their commission is calculated (step by step)
- Their rate table
- How to add new reps, deals, and run commissions
- How disputes work
- Who to contact for support

This document becomes their internal reference and builds trust in the platform.

## Phase 8: Retention & Expansion

### Monthly Check-In (first 3 months)
- "How did last month's commission run go?"
- "Did any reps flag issues?"
- "Is there anything you wish the platform could do?"

### Expansion Triggers
- New sales team or region → configure new rep group or currency
- Commission structure change → update matrix (5-minute job)
- New manager wanting reports → add admin user
- Company acquires another team → new workspace

### Referral Ask (after 60 days)
> "Really glad this has been working well for you. Do you know any other sales ops or finance folks dealing with the same commission headaches? I'd love an intro."
