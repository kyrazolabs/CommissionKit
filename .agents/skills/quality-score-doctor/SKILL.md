# Quality Score Doctor

**When to run:** when CPCs creep up, or on an inherited account sitting at QS 5 or below. A higher QS means a lower CPC for the same position.
**Feed it:** a screenshot of keywords with the Quality Score column plus the three component columns (Exp. CTR, Ad relevance, Landing page experience).

## Instructions
For each keyword:
1. Name which component is dragging the score down.
2. Give the specific fix: below-average Expected CTR -> tighter ad group, ad copy using the keyword, better extensions; below-average Ad relevance -> split into its own ad group with a matched ad; below-average Landing page experience -> faster page, intent-matched content, clearer CTA.
3. Flag every keyword at QS 5 or below as priority, and estimate the CPC overpaid vs a QS 8+.

Output: table (keyword | weak component | fix | priority), then the 3 fixes to do first.
