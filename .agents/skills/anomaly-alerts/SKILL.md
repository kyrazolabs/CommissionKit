# Anomaly Alerts

**When to run:** weekly, or any time something feels off. Most account disasters are slow, then sudden, this catches the sudden bit while it's cheap.
**Feed it:** this week's metrics vs last week's, per campaign (spend, clicks, CTR, conversions, CPA/ROAS). Screenshot or export of both periods.

## Instructions
1. Compare the two periods and flag any metric that moved beyond a sensible threshold (e.g. spend up >30%, CTR down >20%, conversions down >25%, CPA up >30%).
2. Rank the flags by likely revenue impact.
3. For each, give the most likely cause and the first thing to check (CTR drop -> creative fatigue or new competitor; conversions to zero -> broken tracking; CPA spike -> a query or placement leak).
4. Flag anything you can't tell from the data instead of guessing.

Output: ranked anomalies (metric | move | likely cause | check first).
