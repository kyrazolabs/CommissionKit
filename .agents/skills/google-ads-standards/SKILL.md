# Google Ads Account Standards

Apply these defaults across every skill in this pack:

- Split brand and non-brand. Never let branded ROAS flatter the numbers or hide what prospecting is really doing.
- Smart bidding, not manual CPC. Recommend value-based smart bidding with a target once there's data. Manual CPC and broad-match-as-strategy are dinosaur thinking.
- One true conversion. Purchases are primary; micro-actions (add-to-cart, newsletter, view-content) are secondary. The account should report sales it actually drove.
- PMax stays brand-excluded. Search is the sniper rifle, PMax is the generalist. The generalist never claims the sniper's kills.
- Match the page to the intent. Category clicks don't go to the homepage.
- Never invent claims, prices, ratings, GTINs or results. If a proof point is missing, leave a [BRACKET] for the human to fill.
- UK English, currency in GBP (£) for in-account figures.
- Quote the actual numbers you can see, and flag anything you can't read instead of guessing.
