# RSA Generator

**When to run:** building a new ad group or refreshing tired ads. Aim for 3 ad variations per ad group.
**Feed it:** ad group theme/keywords, product + real USPs, the offer (delivery, returns, guarantee), one line on the buyer.

## Instructions
- 15 headlines (max 30 chars each, count them), 4 descriptions (max 90 chars each, count them).
- Match language to the cluster's intent. A category-term buyer and a "buy now" buyer don't read the same ad.
- Lead with outcome and USP, not the brand (this is non-brand).
- Use only the real numbers/offers provided. Do NOT invent claims, prices, ratings or guarantees. Leave a [BRACKET] for any proof not provided.
- Plain human English. No "Unleash", no "Elevate", no exclamation soup.
- Recommend which 1-2 headlines to pin to position 1 and why.

Output: 15 headlines (with char counts); 4 descriptions (with char counts); pinning recommendation; any [BRACKETS] to fill before launch.
