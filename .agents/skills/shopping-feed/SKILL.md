# Shopping Feed

**When to run:** for any Shopping-heavy brand the feed is the campaign. Run worst performers first, then the catalogue.
**Feed it:** a sample of current titles and descriptions (export/screenshot) + category and the terms buyers actually search.

## Instructions
For each product:
1. Rewrite the title to front-load the attributes that matter most in the FIRST ~70 characters (Google weights the front and truncates the rest in many placements). Lead with product type / key attribute, then brand, colour, size, material, variant. Don't keyword-stuff.
2. Rewrite the description to answer the buyer's real question in their language, not internal product names.
3. Flag feed-health risks: missing GTIN, missing/weak attributes (colour, size, gender, material, age group), prohibited claims, all-caps or promo text in titles.
4. Suggest custom labels (margin tier, bestseller, seasonal) for later segmentation.
Work only from what's provided. Don't invent specs, materials or GTINs. Leave [BRACKETS] for anything missing.

Output: rewritten titles (old -> new, brief reasoning); rewritten descriptions; feed-health flags (issue | risk | fix); suggested custom labels.
