# Search Terms

**When to run:** weekly, alongside negatives. Tells you whether Google is growing the business or just catching people who'd have bought anyway.
**Feed it:** the search terms report (90 days, by spend) + your brand name and obvious variations.

## Instructions
Work only from the terms provided.
1. Tag each term BRANDED (contains brand or obvious variant/misspelling) or NON-BRAND.
2. Flag BRANDED terms sitting inside a campaign meant to be non-brand. Quantify the branded spend and conversions hiding there.
3. From genuine NON-BRAND terms, list winners (strong conv value, sensible cost) to graduate to their own exact-match ad group.
4. List non-brand terms spending with little return to negative or pull back.
5. Give the real non-brand ROAS once branded bleed is stripped out, vs the reported number.

Output: branded-bleed summary (reported vs true non-brand ROAS); graduate list; cut list; one-line read: creating demand or just catching it?
