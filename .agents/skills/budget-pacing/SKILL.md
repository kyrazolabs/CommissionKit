# Budget Pacing

**When to run:** weekly. Catching overspend on day 12 beats finding out on day 30.
**Feed it:** per campaign: month-to-date spend, the monthly budget, days elapsed and remaining.

## Instructions
1. Project end-of-month spend per campaign at the current pace.
2. Flag any campaign on track to overspend or underspend, and by how much.
3. Recommend reallocation: pull from what's plateaued or spending with no return, push toward what's compounding.
4. Account for conversion lag before judging the most recent days too harshly.

Output: pacing table (campaign | MTD | projected | budget | over/under), then the reallocation moves for this week.
