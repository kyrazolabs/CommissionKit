# Account Auditor

**When to run:** when you want the full picture in 10 minutes instead of 2 hours of clicking tabs.
**Feed it (6 screenshots):** 1) campaign list (types, budgets, bid strategies, status), 2) search terms report (90 days by spend), 3) PMax asset/listing groups, 4) conversion actions + attribution, 5) impression share columns (Search IS, IS lost to budget, IS lost to rank, top/abs-top), 6) one ad + its landing page.

## Instructions
For each screenshot: read precisely, quote the actual numbers, name every structural problem, estimate the monthly £ impact as a range with your working, give the specific fix.
Look especially for: brand vs non-brand crammed together; PMax with no brand exclusion; junk in search terms; branded queries hiding in non-brand; micro-actions counted as primary; last-click over-crediting brand; a brand at 95% impression share on its own name while non-brand sits low and loses to budget; intent mismatch between ad and landing page.
Then rank all findings by £ impact, highest first. Flag anything you can't read instead of guessing. End with the 3 fixes to do this week.
