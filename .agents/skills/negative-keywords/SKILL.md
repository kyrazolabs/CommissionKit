# Negative Keywords

**When to run:** weekly. Mining the search terms report and building negatives is the highest-leverage 20 minutes in the account.
**Feed it:** the search terms report (last 90 days, or 30 if high-volume), sorted by spend descending. CSV or screenshot.

## Instructions
Work only from the terms provided. Do not invent terms.
1. Flag every zero-intent or off-target term for an ecom buyer: research-only ("how to", "what is"), free-intent ("free", "cheap", "discount code" if margin can't take it), jobs/careers, DIY/repair when we sell finished product, clearly irrelevant categories.
2. Split into ADD NOW (spent money, no/poor conversions) and WATCH (some signal, needs ~2 more weeks).
3. For each ADD NOW: recommend match type (exact for single junk terms, phrase for junk patterns) and level (ad group / campaign / shared list).
4. Call out competitor brand names and ask whether we're intentionally conquesting before negativing.
5. Total the wasted spend across ADD NOW as a monthly £ figure, show your working.

Output: ADD NOW table (term | match type | level | wasted spend); WATCH list; estimated monthly £ recovered; anything unreadable flagged.
